Bobalytics

Track Bob's impact on your SDLC with three KPIs — available exclusively on the Enterprise plan.

Bobalytics · Patterns · KPIs LIVE
ADOPTION Adoption rate
Average daily active users ÷ total licensed seats. A user is active when they accept a code completion or complete a chat conversation. Drill into usage levels (daily, weekly, occasional, inactive) to spot who needs enablement.
VALUE Bob factor
Lines of code committed by Bob ÷ total lines committed. Measures Bob's tangible contribution to shipped code. Pair with Bobcoin spend — high factor + low spend = high efficiency.
COST Bobcoin spend
Total Bobcoins consumed over the selected period, broken down by team and day. High spend + low Bob factor = opportunity to review how Bob is being used within that team.
